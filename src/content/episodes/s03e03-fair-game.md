## Why It Matters

Earth is increasingly difficult for the Goa'uld to treat as an insignificant rebellious world. The episode moves the Tau'ri into the political calculations of much older galactic powers and strengthens the importance of Earth's relationship with the Asgard.

## Rewatch Notes

The fun is not simply the diplomatic setup. It is the contrast between the scale of the negotiation and O'Neill's obvious discomfort at being used as a diplomat.

## Character Dynamic

O'Neill is at his best when command requires restraint rather than firepower. Thor's confidence in him makes the situation even better.
