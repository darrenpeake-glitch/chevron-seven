## Rewatch Notes

The fun is not simply the diplomatic setup. It is the contrast between the scale of the negotiation and O'Neill's obvious discomfort at being used as a diplomat.

## Character Dynamic

O'Neill is at his best when command requires restraint rather than firepower. Thor's confidence in him makes the situation even better.
