## Why It Matters

*Legacy* shifts attention away from conventional off-world threats and puts Daniel at the center of a story about uncertainty, perception and the residue left behind by Goa'uld power.

It also reinforces a recurring Stargate idea: ancient or apparently abandoned technology can remain dangerous long after the people who created or used it are gone.

## Rewatch Notes

This is a useful episode for watching how SG-1 behaves when the threat cannot be solved simply by identifying an enemy and shooting it.

On a rewatch, the team dynamics are at least as interesting as the mystery itself.

## Character Dynamic

Daniel's relationship with the rest of SG-1 does much of the work here. The episode tests how seriously the team takes him when his experience cannot initially be verified by anyone else.

## Things to Watch

- How the episode frames Daniel's reliability.
- The difference between medical, scientific and military interpretations of the problem.
- How quickly SG-1's normal certainty disappears when evidence becomes subjective.
