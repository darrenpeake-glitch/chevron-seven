## Overview

The Asgard are an extremely advanced extraterrestrial civilization with technology far beyond both Earth and the Goa'uld. Their intervention on Earth's behalf gives the Tau'ri a level of protection and political significance they could not yet create on their own.

## Relationship with Earth

The relationship is cautious but increasingly important. Thor in particular treats SG-1 as more than a curiosity, and Asgard involvement forces the Goa'uld to consider Earth within a much larger diplomatic framework.

## Established Capabilities

- Interstellar travel at extraordinary speed.
- Highly advanced transport technology.
- Powerful holographic and communication systems.
- Technology capable of intimidating the Goa'uld System Lords.

## Strategic Importance

For Earth, the Asgard are not merely powerful allies. Their willingness to engage diplomatically changes how other galactic powers calculate the cost of acting against the Tau'ri.
