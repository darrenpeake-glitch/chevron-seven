## Overview

The Goa'uld are parasitic beings that take human hosts and rule large populations through a mixture of military force, advanced technology and religious mythology. Many pose as gods drawn from human history and maintain power through fear and technological superiority.

## Structure

Goa'uld power is fragmented among competing System Lords rather than controlled by a single unified empire. Alliances are temporary, rivalries are constant, and internal politics can be as dangerous as open warfare.

## Relationship with Earth

Earth is increasingly difficult for the Goa'uld to dismiss as an isolated primitive world. SG-1 repeatedly disrupts their plans, and the Tau'ri's growing relationships with other advanced powers complicate the balance further.

## Established Characteristics

- Depend on human hosts.
- Use Jaffa as soldiers and enforcers.
- Frequently adopt divine identities.
- Possess technology far beyond contemporary Earth systems.
- Compete intensely with one another for territory and influence.
