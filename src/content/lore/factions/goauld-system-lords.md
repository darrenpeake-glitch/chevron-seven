## Profile

The Goa'uld System Lords are the most powerful Goa'uld rulers encountered by Stargate Command. They control large territories, military forces and subject populations, but they are not a unified government in the human sense. Rivalry, status and self-interest shape their relationships with one another.

## Political Structure

Their power depends on control: worlds, armies, resources and reputation. Cooperation is possible when a common threat is serious enough, but every agreement sits inside a wider contest for advantage.

## Relationship with Earth

By **Fair Game**, Earth has become troublesome enough to enter System Lord diplomacy directly. That shift matters: the Tau'ri are no longer merely rebels to be punished, but a strategic problem that requires collective attention.

## Established Characteristics

- Highly competitive despite shared Goa'uld interests.
- Rule through military strength, intimidation and claims of divinity.
- Possess technology far beyond Earth's conventional military capability.
- Treat political status and perceived weakness as strategically important.
