## Overview

A Stargate is a ring-shaped transportation device capable of creating a stable wormhole to another Stargate. A destination is selected by dialing a sequence of symbols that functions as an address.

## Operational Principle

The sending gate establishes the wormhole and matter travels one way through the active connection. The network allows near-instant movement between worlds that would otherwise require interstellar spacecraft.

## Strategic Importance

The Stargate changes the scale of Earth's reach completely. With a single installation beneath Cheyenne Mountain, Stargate Command can explore other planets, form alliances, recover technology and encounter threats across the galaxy.

## Established Characteristics

- Destinations are selected through symbolic gate addresses.
- Connections require enormous but manageable energy.
- Matter can pass through an established wormhole.
- Gates form part of a much older transportation network.
- Control of a gate can determine access to an entire world.
