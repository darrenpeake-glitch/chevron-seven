## Profile

Teal'c is a Jaffa warrior who abandoned his position as First Prime of Apophis and joined the Tau'ri. His knowledge of Goa'uld military culture, technology and command structure makes him indispensable to SG-1.

## Role in SG-1

He is both frontline combatant and cultural intelligence source. Where Earth personnel often encounter Goa'uld systems without context, Teal'c can identify ranks, customs, technology and likely tactical behavior from experience.

## Character Dynamics

**Jack O'Neill:** mutual trust expressed with remarkable efficiency. O'Neill's humor and Teal'c's restraint create one of SG-1's most distinctive dynamics.

**Daniel Jackson:** Daniel often approaches Jaffa belief and history through questions; Teal'c supplies the lived experience behind the mythology.

**Samantha Carter:** a highly practical working relationship built around competence and shared mission focus.

## Established Traits

- Highly disciplined and physically formidable.
- Speaks with unusual precision.
- Carries deep knowledge of Goa'uld society.
- Committed to the possibility of Jaffa freedom.
- Increasingly curious about Earth customs.
