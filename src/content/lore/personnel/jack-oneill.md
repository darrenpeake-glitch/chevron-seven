## Profile

O'Neill commands SG-1 in the field and usually approaches problems from the practical end first: identify the threat, protect the team and find a way home. He is an experienced Air Force officer, but his leadership style is deliberately informal and often masks how carefully he is reading a situation.

## Role in SG-1

He is the team's tactical lead and final field authority. Carter supplies much of the scientific analysis, Daniel provides linguistic and archaeological context, and Teal'c brings direct knowledge of the Goa'uld. O'Neill's job is often to turn those different perspectives into a decision quickly enough to keep everyone alive.

## Character Dynamics

**Daniel Jackson:** close friendship complicated by radically different instincts. Daniel tends to ask what a culture means; O'Neill tends to ask whether it is about to shoot them.

**Samantha Carter:** strong professional trust. O'Neill relies heavily on Carter's technical judgment even when he pretends not to follow the explanation.

**Teal'c:** unusually economical friendship. Their mutual respect is often expressed with very little dialogue.

## Established Traits

- Uses humor and sarcasm under pressure.
- Deeply suspicious of Goa'uld motives.
- Protective of his team.
- Frequently impatient with ceremony, politics and excessive explanation.
- More perceptive than his deliberately casual manner suggests.
