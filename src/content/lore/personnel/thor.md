## Profile

Thor is an Asgard representative with direct authority to speak and act on matters involving Earth. His interactions with SG-1 make clear that the Asgard evaluate humanity on a scale far beyond Earth's own politics.

## Relationship with Earth

By this point in the rewatch, Thor treats Earth as a world worth protecting and SG-1 as people capable of affecting wider galactic events. His confidence in O'Neill is particularly significant because it places an ordinary human officer inside negotiations between powers vastly older and more advanced than the Tau'ri.

## Established Traits

- Calm and highly controlled in communication.
- Possesses access to technology far beyond Earth's capabilities.
- Willing to intervene when larger strategic interests justify it.
- Shows unusual trust in SG-1, especially O'Neill.
