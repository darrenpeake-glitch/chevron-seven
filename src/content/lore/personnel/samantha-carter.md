## Profile

Carter is SG-1's principal scientific specialist and one of the people most capable of translating unfamiliar technology into something the team can actually use. Her expertise spans astrophysics, engineering and Stargate-related phenomena, but she is also a serving Air Force officer expected to make decisions under field conditions.

## Role in SG-1

She is usually the first member of the team to turn an impossible-looking phenomenon into a testable problem. O'Neill often depends on Carter to tell him what is happening, how dangerous it is and whether there is a way out.

## Character Dynamics

**Jack O'Neill:** a disciplined professional relationship built on strong trust. O'Neill frequently cuts short the explanation while still relying on Carter's conclusion.

**Daniel Jackson:** complementary problem-solvers. Carter tends toward physical mechanisms while Daniel often supplies historical, linguistic or cultural context.

**Teal'c:** mutual professional respect, especially when Goa'uld technology or military capability is involved.

## Established Traits

- Highly analytical under pressure.
- Comfortable moving between laboratory reasoning and field action.
- Frequently responsible for explaining why the current situation is scientifically alarming.
- Strong sense of duty to both SG-1 and the wider mission of Stargate Command.
