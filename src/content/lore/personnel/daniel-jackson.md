## Profile

Daniel is SG-1's specialist in ancient cultures, languages and mythology. He approaches the Stargate network as evidence of connected civilizations rather than simply a military environment, which often puts him in the position of asking questions the rest of the team might otherwise skip.

## Role in SG-1

He decodes inscriptions, identifies cultural patterns and frequently provides the historical context that turns an apparently isolated encounter into part of a much larger picture. His presence gives SG-1 a diplomatic and archaeological capability that a purely military team would not have.

## Character Dynamics

**Jack O'Neill:** close friendship built around constant disagreement over method. Daniel wants to understand first; O'Neill wants to know whether everyone is safe first.

**Samantha Carter:** naturally collaborative, particularly when ancient evidence and modern science begin pointing toward the same answer.

**Teal'c:** Daniel often values Teal'c's first-hand knowledge of Goa'uld society while bringing an outsider's willingness to question assumptions around it.

## Established Traits

- Exceptionally strong linguistic ability.
- Deep curiosity about unfamiliar cultures.
- Persistent moral concern for people encountered through the Stargate.
- Willingness to challenge military assumptions when he thinks important context is being missed.
