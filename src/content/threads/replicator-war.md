## Thread Overview

This thread tracks only episodes that materially establish, develop, escalate, reveal or resolve the Replicator conflict. Mere appearances or unrelated Asgard stories do not automatically qualify.

## Rewatch Reading

The earliest contributions can be extremely small. A single line may function as a breadcrumb long before the audience understands its importance. Later episodes turn those hints into an explicit conflict, then continue to develop the threat and its consequences.

## Editorial Rule

An episode belongs here only when removing it would meaningfully weaken the viewer's understanding of the Replicator storyline. Primary entries are essential to the thread; secondary entries contribute a meaningful clue, consequence or piece of context.
